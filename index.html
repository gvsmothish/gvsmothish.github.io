2025 Google PhD Fellow · Machine Perception

I'm a third-year PhD student at CDS, IISc Bangalore, advised by Dr. Deepak Subramani. I work on generative models, computer vision, neural operators and reasoning in foundation models.

Before my PhD, I did an MTech in Robotics and Autonomous Systems at RBCCPS, IISc, with Dr. Shishir N. Y. at the Stochastic Robotics Lab, building RL- and diffusion-based locomotion for walking robots. I'm broadly interested in intelligent stochastic systems.
